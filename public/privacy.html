Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

1. What we collect

We collect the data you provide when you create an account: your email address, display name, and age confirmation. We also collect the receipt images and metadata you upload while using SnapBooks.

2. Purpose — why we use your data

We use your data in order to provide the SnapBooks service: to authenticate you, store your receipts, and allow you to retrieve them. We do not sell your data to third parties and do not use it for advertising.

3. How long we retain data

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, all data associated with it — including your receipts and profile — is permanently deleted within 30 days. You may also request deletion at any time.

4. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from your Profile page. Deleting your account permanently removes your email, display name, and all uploaded receipts from our systems. This action cannot be undone.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in the EU West region (West Europe) on Supabase infrastructure. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this notice, contact us at privacy@snapbooks.app.
